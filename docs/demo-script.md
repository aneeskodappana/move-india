# Vandi demo script (≤3 minutes)

Narration for a screen-recorded walkthrough of https://vandi-eight.vercel.app. Spoken length is about 430 words.

## 0:00–0:30 — Cold open

I paid my waste collection fee every month. One day I missed a pickup by minutes, left my bag where I was told to, and a stray dog got to it before anyone collected it. I got fined ₹10,000 — because there was no record of what I did or when.

## 0:30–1:00 — Why it happens

The official system registers the property, not the person living in it. In a rental-heavy city, the owner is in the WhatsApp group. The tenant is invisible. The schedule changes daily. When handover happens at an informal staging point, accountability disappears.

## 1:00–2:30 — The journey

This is Vandi, an independent hackathon prototype on synthetic Kochi data. No government system is connected.

Anjali is a tenant at Demo Lotus House. She signs in with a clearly labeled DEV MODE code — not a real SMS.

Today she is told, unambiguously: food waste, 7:00 to 8:30 AM, on the Elamkulam North route. The same sentence is previewed as an app push, an SMS, and a WhatsApp bubble. Those channels are simulated. Nothing is sent.

She marks kept out. That writes her timestamp. A separate collector session — not her login — marks collected. Both times stay on the record.

History is the proof pack: date, material, her kept-out time, the collector time. 13 August is the gap the pitch is about — she marked it, the collector mark is missing. That is the artifact that would have made a wrongful fine indefensible.

Payments are hers, not the household’s. Pay ₹80 is mock UPI. No real money moves. The receipt is still a personal record she can open and print.

## 2:30–3:00 — Close

This is not a nicer notification. It changes the unit of identity from the house to the occupant.

A real deployment would need LSGD or Suchitwa Mission, and real telecom. Until then, the working prototype is here: vandi-eight.vercel.app.
